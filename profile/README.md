## What we do

**Platform.** Ranks candidates against tiered requirements from your job description and flags suspect profiles for human review. Integrates with any ATS.

**Recruiting as a service.** Our recruiters run the search in Pairwise. You follow the pipeline and give feedback in the same place.

**Managed services.** Vetted engineers assigned to your project under a SOW, across robotics, RF, sensors, silicon and audio.

Recruiters make every decision. Pairwise sorts the pile so they spend their time on the right people.

## Built by operators

Our team ran an engineering services firm for 10 years, filling hard roles in embedded, hardware, software and semiconductor. We built Pairwise around that work.

## Work from your AI assistant
